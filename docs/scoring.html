Every score is worked out from the stored battle results, using the robots on the participant list. A melee battle counts as one result for each pair of robots in it.

APS (Average Percentage Score)
In one result, a robot's share of the two scores: 100 * score / (score + opponent's score). A pairing's APS is the average over its results, and a robot's APS is the average over its pairings.
APS CI
The half width of the 95% confidence interval on the APS. It uses each pairing's variance between battles, pulled towards a variance of 16 as if there were 3 extra battles, so that a pairing with few battles does not look certain.
PWIN
The percentage of pairings the robot wins, meaning an APS above 50. An exact 50 counts as half a win.
Vote
Each robot gives one vote to the opponent that scores best against it, split evenly on a tie. A robot's Vote is the percentage of its opponents that voted for it.
NPP (Normalised Percentage Pairing)
A robot's APS against an opponent, scaled so that the lowest score any robot gets against that opponent is 0 and the highest is 100. ANPP is the average NPP over a robot's pairings.
KNNPBI (K-Nearest-Neighbours Problem Bot Index)
A robot's APS against an opponent, minus the average APS of the robots near it in the rankings against that opponent. A negative number marks a problem bot: an opponent this robot does worse against than robots of about the same strength. The neighbours are about half the square root of the number of robots on each side.
Min APS
The robot's lowest APS in any one result of the pairing.
Survival
The percentage of rounds the robot finished first, averaged over its pairings.

These definitions follow LiteRumble. Unlike LiteRumble, scores here have no separate batch step and are all recomputed together every few seconds.
